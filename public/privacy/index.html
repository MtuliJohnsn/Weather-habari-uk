Habari Weather Privacy Policy

Habari Weather is a free, zero-ad, zero-subscription weather observatory and commute micro-weather tool.

1. Location & GPS Data

Saved cities and temperature units are stored locally on your device via browser storage. If you choose to tap My GPS or Pin GPS, your coordinates are sent directly to open weather and routing feeds solely to display local conditions and road weather. We do not track background location.

2. Optional Google Sign-In & Community Reports

Signing in with Google via Firebase Authentication is optional and only required if you post or verify crowdsourced Ground Truth hazard tags or sync custom threshold alert rules across devices. Active Ground Truth pins show your display name and automatically expire after 6 hours.

3. Free & Open Data Sources ($0)

  • Forecasts & Multi-Model Confidence: Open-Meteo (GFS, ECMWF, ICON) & NOAA/National Weather Service
  • Live Precipitation Radar: RainViewer Public Weather Maps
  • Map Tiles, Search & Road Routing: OpenStreetMap contributors, CARTO Dark Matter basemaps, Photon Geocoder, and OSRM
